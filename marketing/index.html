One contract · one gate · one road

How the matrix works

No agent talks to another directly. A request enters through HOPE, is routed by APEX, checked by KNOLL, and only then reaches DREAM (simulate) or VISION (execute). Results return the same way. The separation of concerns is enforced in code, not suggested in a prompt.

Credibility is the moat

Honest by design

We sell the architecture, not a fantasy weight count. Every claim on this page is meant to survive an engineer's first question.

What "~14.3 quadrillion parameters" actually means

It's 20,480 nodes × 100 personas × 7B — a measure of addressable persona-capacity the topology can express, not 14.3Q trained weights you can download. The honest, economical realization is shared base weights + cheap per-persona deltas, and only the active personas ever cost GPU. That's why your bill tracks active-param-seconds, and why an idle matrix costs almost nothing.
